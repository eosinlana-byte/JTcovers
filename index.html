About

The Designer Behind the Covers

I’m Joanna Thompson, founder of JT Cover Studio. I design book covers that sell the feeling of a story before a single page is read.

Authors come to me when they need a cover that looks traditionally published: strong type, a clear mood, and a design that holds up as a tiny thumbnail and a full print wrap.

Every project starts with your genre, your reader, and the one image that should stop the scroll. Then we refine until it feels inevitable.

Focus
Custom covers and series branding that look ready for the shelf.
Approach
Story first. Then type, color, and composition that match the shelf.
For
Indie and hybrid authors who want their book to look like it belongs next to the bestsellers.

The process

  1. 01 Brief

    You send the story, genre, comps, and what you love (and don’t).

  2. 02 Concept
